- Tier 2 data is higher-confidence structured evidence but may be INCOMPLETE — use it as a starting point, and actively look for additional features in the image that GeoSym may have missed.
- Tier 3 data is inferred from limited points — treat it as a hint only. Always verify and extend using the image.

(c) Identify any local maxima or minima (extrema) visible in the graph.

    - Carefully check the image for any ADDITIONAL extrema not detected by GeoSym.
    - Give each as {x, y, type} where type is "max" or "min".
